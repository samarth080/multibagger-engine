# P2.1 — Business-Quality / Franchise-Durability Engine + Living Thesis + Self-Critique

**Date:** 2026-07-12 · **Status:** Approved (autonomous mode, Phase 2)
**Goal:** Make the platform reason about a *decade-long business trajectory* and
form a *falsifiable, self-critiqued investment thesis* — then prove the
franchise-durability signal carries forward-return information via ablation.

## Why this is the highest-value first move

Phase-1 scoring reads a mostly single-year snapshot. Great long-term investors
reason over the whole history: *has this business compounded capital at high
rates consistently, survived downturns, converted profit to cash, and avoided
diluting owners — for years?* That is a different, richer question, computable
deterministically from the multi-year statement history we already ingest
(EDGAR 15-20y, NSE/Yahoo 5y+). And it is directly ablatable.

## Components

### 1. `analysis/business.py` — Business-Quality / Franchise engine
`assess_business(fin, info, fund) -> BusinessProfile`, pure. Over ALL available
history:
- **Durability**
  - `roce_track`: median, min, and fraction of years with ROCE ≥ 15%
  - `growth_track`: fraction of years with positive YoY revenue growth; revenue
    5y+ CAGR consistency
  - `margin_stability`: 1 − coefficient of variation of operating margin (pricing power)
  - `margin_trajectory`: OLS slope of operating margin over years (expanding/compressing)
  - `earnings_quality_track`: fraction of years with CFO/NI ≥ 0.8
- **Compounding engine**
  - `incremental_roic`: Σ ΔEBIT·(1−tax) / Σ Δinvested_capital across the window
    (return earned on reinvested capital — the true compounding rate)
- **Resilience**
  - `worst_revenue_drawdown`: largest peak-to-trough revenue decline in history
  - `ever_lossmaking`: any year with negative net income
  - `survived_downturn`: spans ≥1 revenue-down year yet stayed profitable
- **Classification** (deterministic label): `Durable Compounder` / `Steady` /
  `Cyclical` / `Turnaround` / `Deteriorating` / `Unproven` (short history)
- `franchise_score` 0-100 (the ablation candidate), with an Evidence trail.
- `history_years`, `completeness`.

### 2. `thesis/engine.py` — Living Investment Thesis with falsifiable assumptions
`build_thesis(info, fund, business, val, risk) -> InvestmentThesis`:
- `business_summary` (what it does — sector/industry/description, trimmed)
- `bull_pillars`: durable strengths found (from franchise evidence)
- `assumptions: list[Assumption]` — each: `statement`, `historical_support`
  (probability from the company's OWN track record, e.g. fraction of years the
  condition held), `currently_true` (latest year). Examples: sustains ROCE≥15%,
  grows revenue, maintains/expands margins, converts profit to cash, avoids
  dilution, controls leverage.
- `falsifiers`: what would break the thesis (the inverse of the key assumptions)
- `trajectory`: bull / base / bear business paths (narrative + which assumptions hold)
- `thesis_confidence`: from assumption support × data completeness × history length

### 3. `thesis/critique.py` — Self-critique / Devil's advocate
`critique_thesis(thesis, fund, business, val, risk) -> Critique`. Actively
enumerates disconfirming evidence (assumptions currently false, margin
compression, rising leverage, dilution, weak earnings quality, valuation
implying growth far above delivered, high risk score, deteriorating
classification). Produces `disconfirmers: list[str]` and `veto: bool`. A thesis
is only "actionable" after surviving critique; a veto downgrades the
recommendation to "Pass — [reason]". Embodies the directive: *attempt to reject
before recommending.*

### 4. Persistence + change detection (`storage.py`)
`save_thesis(ticker, thesis, as_of)` and `thesis_history(ticker)`. On rerun,
`diff_thesis(prev, curr)` reports which assumptions strengthened/weakened/flipped
— longitudinal memory (seeds P2.2).

### 5. Wiring
- `pipeline.analyze_ticker` attaches `business` + `thesis` + `critique` to the bundle.
- Report gains a **Business & Thesis** section: classification, franchise
  evidence, assumptions table (with historical-support probabilities),
  falsifiers, self-critique disconfirmers, and the post-critique recommendation.
- Web terminal shows the thesis on the report page.

### 6. VALIDATION (the module's right to exist)
`scripts/ablation_franchise.py`: run the multi-score harness with
`["multibagger", "franchise", "Quality"]` on cached US small-cap + India
universes, 2y horizon. Report each score's IC. **Decision rule:** franchise
stays a first-class score only if its IC is ≥ the base multibagger IC on the
majority of samples; otherwise it is documented as a null and demoted to a
descriptive (non-scoring) business profile. Either outcome is recorded honestly.

## Testing
Hand-computed anchors for every business metric (incremental ROIC, margin
stability CV, drawdown, assumption probabilities); classification boundary
tests; critique veto tests (clean compounder → no veto; deteriorating levered
diluter → veto); thesis persistence + diff round-trip; report contains the new
section. No regressions in the 100-test suite.

## Non-goals (later increments)
Management/guidance tracking (P2.3), document/con-call NLP (P2.4), causal graph
(P2.5). This increment establishes the business-trajectory + thesis + critique
spine and proves whether franchise durability carries signal.
