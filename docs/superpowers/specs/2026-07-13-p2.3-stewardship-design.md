# P2.3 — Management & Capital-Allocation Intelligence (Stewardship)

**Date:** 2026-07-13 · **Status:** Approved (autonomous mode, Phase 2)
**Goal:** Judge management by its multi-year capital-allocation record — the
part of "management quality" that is deterministic and auditable from
statements. No con-call adjectives; only what they *did* with owners' money.

## Signals (all full-history, hand-testable)

| Signal | Computation | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| Dilution discipline | full-history share CAGR; fraction of years diluting > 2%; buyback years | do owners get diluted or bought back? |
| Allocation fit | incremental ROIC x reinvestment rate 2x2: high-ROIC reinvestor = compounding machine; low-ROIC heavy reinvestor = **empire builder**; low-ROIC cash returner = disciplined | is capital routed to where returns are? |
| Debt discipline | CAGR(debt) - CAGR(EBIT); fraction of years with coverage < 2 | does leverage fund returns or mask decay? |
| Shareholder returns | dividend-paying consistency; share-count shrink years | cash actually returned |

-> `StewardshipProfile`: metrics + evidence trail + `stewardship_score` (0-100)
+ classification: `Owner-Operator Discipline / Balanced / Empire Builder /
Serial Diluter / Unproven`.

## Wiring
Pipeline bundle + report section (inside Business & Thesis) + harness score
name `stewardship` + one new thesis bull-pillar/disconfirmer hook.

## Validation (pre-registered, same as P2.1)
Ablation on the cached US x2 + India panels, 2y horizon: `stewardship` stays a
first-class score only if IC >= base multibagger in the majority of samples;
otherwise demoted to descriptive-only (like franchise). Either way recorded.
Given P2.1's outcome, the descriptive/report value must stand on its own:
empire-builder and serial-diluter labels are risk disclosures investors want
surfaced regardless of IC.
